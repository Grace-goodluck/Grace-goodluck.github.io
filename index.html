About

I am Grace Ihuoma Nwakama, founder of TechByGrace, based in Lagos, Nigeria and available for remote, hybrid and on-site roles. Since 2024 I have built websites, web apps, databases and AI agents for clients and for my own portfolio.

Second Brain (AI agent)

Python · FastAPI · Gemini API · DuckDB

A retrieval-augmented question-answering agent over the DuckDB documentation. It answers only from its sources, cites every claim, and says "not found" when the answer is not in the documents. It refused every unanswerable question in evaluation. An agent loop rewrites failed searches and improved correct answers from 10 to 11 out of 12 against the baseline pipeline. Built test-first with 269 automated tests.
